# Image credits

Photos on the drive pages from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/). Each one was chosen under these rules, and approved by the site owner before it was downloaded on 7 November 2026:

- Licence CC0, public domain or CC BY only: no NonCommercial, NoDerivatives or ShareAlike licences.
- No restrictions noted on the file page (personality rights, trademarks, "not free in all countries"). The four public domain files are released by their photographers (PD-self and PD-user templates).
- The photo shows what its caption says, checked against the file description and categories, and shows no recognisable people.
- Landscape, at least 2000 px wide in the original, with no watermark.

Each file was downloaded as a resized copy from Commons, then resized to 1600 px and 800 px wide and saved as WebP. For CC BY photos the on-page credit says "resized", as the licences ask you to say when you change a work.

The credit (photographer and licence, linked to the Commons page) is shown under each photo, and the same list is on the About page.
